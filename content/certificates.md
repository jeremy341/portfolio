## Internship at the University of Duisburg-Essen

- Issued by: University of Duisburg-Essen, Faculty of Engineering, Electronic Components and Circuits (EBS)
- Kind: Internship reference (Praktikumszeugnis)
- Date: Internship 3 to 28 August 2026, letter dated 7 September 2026
- Image: certificates/ude-internship.jpg
- Image description: Second page of the internship reference from the University of Duisburg-Essen for Jeremy Darko, signed by Prof. Dr.-Ing. Karsten Seidl. The line with the date and place of birth is covered by a black bar.

The internship was voluntary and lasted four weeks, in the EBS field of study, where I worked on a control and image-analysis system for droplet-based microfluidic systems. My part was this:

- integrating different hardware and software components into one system, and building the graphical user interface to run and evaluate it
- running experiments and helping to commission, validate and optimise the system
- developing methods for automatic image analysis, in particular for detecting and measuring microdroplets
- documenting the software, the integration and the experiments, and evaluating the results

The work became [FluidicStudio](projects/fluidicstudio.md). I only show the reference page, because the cover letter has an address and bank details, so I left it out. The line with my date and place of birth is covered by a bar that's part of the image itself.

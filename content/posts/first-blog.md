## What have I been doing?

Let me start with what I have been doing the last few months. So for one I have been actively taking part in [Stardance](https://stardance.hackclub.com/), a [YSWS](https://project.hackclub.com/overview/info/) run by [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com), a nonprofit for tech-oriented teenagers that rewards them for coding or building hardware projects (shoutout [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com)).

During Stardance, I created [Poorup](/work/poorup/), my biggest and coolest project to date. Pls check it out.

And because of Poorup, I even managed to get a [Bambu Lab P1S](https://bambulab.com/) totally for free. Crazy, right?

After actively being part of the community, I thought, "Now I wanna somehow organize my own event and give back." So I decided to start [LOADOUT](https://loadout.dino.icu), a proposal YSWS organized by me, which would run through [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com) (shoutout Ar***d). It will allow people to come to Germany for free for a three-day hackathon if they manage to create enough projects. So I can confidently say that because of [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com) my last few months have been pretty dope, though also pretty exhausting.


## [Loadout](https://loadout.dino.icu) planning

So I just noticed I should probably explain [Loadout](https://loadout.dino.icu) in a bit more detail and what I have done, so here I am, ik nice from me, isn't it :). What is [Loadout](https://loadout.dino.icu)? Well, I noticed that most [YSWS](https://project.hackclub.com/overview/info/) go in one of two directions: they're either focused on one specific kind of project, or they allow pretty much anything.

A focused YSWS can help you build a specific skill, but not everyone can take part. A general one is open to more people, but might not help them go deeper in one area. Loadout plans to offer multiple areas while rewarding people for focusing on one.

But let me explain what I needed to do. Basically I was tasked with creating a rough cost spreadsheet for the proposal, and I really struggled with how expensive stuff is. Don't get me wrong, it was fun, but I definitely got a reality check.

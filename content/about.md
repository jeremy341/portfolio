## Short profile

I'm Jeremy, a high school student from NRW building projects in computer engineering, machine learning, GPU computing and hardware.

## Biography

My work includes [MIRA](/work/mira/), a camera model that sorts waste, [TorchVK](/work/torchvk/), my research into training on an old AMD GPU, and [Poorup](/work/poorup/), a browser game. In 2026 I spent four weeks at the University of Duisburg-Essen working on control software for microfluidic systems, which is also where [FluidicStudio](/work/fluidicstudio/) started. My skills are Python, C++, TypeScript and PyTorch, with embedded work on ESP32 boards and GPU compute with Vulkan. I'm looking for an internship in the Easter holidays of 2027, and my goal is to build machine learning systems that run on real hardware, not just in notebooks. I keep track of what I am doing right now on [my now page](/now/).
